# Bohloko Family Farm – Poultry Department  
**Integrated Poultry Farming, Processing & Distribution**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Bohloko Family Farm – Poultry Department is a community-based agricultural enterprise located in the Free State, South Africa. The business focuses on the production, slaughtering, processing, and distribution of high-quality poultry products for Bloemfontein, surrounding townships, and the broader Free State market. The project aims to strengthen food security, create local employment, and stimulate the township economy through sustainable poultry production.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Bloemfontein and surrounding townships experience high unemployment, rising food prices, and limited participation in agricultural value chains. Most poultry consumed in the area is transported from other provinces, leading to high costs, unstable supply, and limited local economic benefit. There is a critical need for a locally rooted poultry production and processing enterprise that ensures affordable access to quality protein while creating jobs and economic opportunities within the Free State.

---

## 3. Business Objectives

- Produce affordable, high-quality poultry products
- Establish a certified slaughtering and processing facility
- Supply Bloemfontein city and township markets
- Create sustainable employment opportunities
- Strengthen local food security
- Achieve profitability within 12–18 months

---

## 4. Products and Services

### Core Products
- Live broiler chickens  
- Whole dressed chickens  
- Chicken portions (breast, thighs, wings, drumsticks)  
- Frozen packaged chicken  

### Value-Added Products
- Marinated chicken portions  
- Smoked chicken  
- Chicken gizzards and offal  
- Chicken feet

### Sales Channels
- Farm gate sales  
- Local markets and butcheries  
- Restaurants and fast-food outlets  
- Online orders and home delivery

---

## 5. Market Analysis

### Target Customers
- Urban and township households  
- Spaza shops and butcheries  
- Restaurants and caterers  
- Schools, hospitals, and institutions  
- Event service providers

### Market Opportunity

Poultry remains South Africa’s most consumed protein source. Demand in the Free State continues to grow while local production and processing capacity remains limited, creating strong market potential for a stable, locally produced poultry supply.

## 6. Operations Plan

### Production Cycle
- Broiler growth period: 5–6 weeks  
- Starting capacity: 500–1000 birds per cycle  
- 6–8 production cycles per year

### Facilities

- Feed storage  
- Slaughtering and processing room  
- Cold storage  
- Delivery vehicle

### Staffing
- Farm manager  
- Poultry attendants  
- Processing staff
