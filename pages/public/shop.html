How to Order

Simple steps to get fresh poultry delivered to your door.

1. Contact Us

Call, WhatsApp, or email us with your product requirements and quantities.

2. Get a Quote

We'll provide a detailed quote with pricing, bulk discounts, and delivery options.

3. Confirm & Pay

Confirm your order and choose payment: cash on delivery, EFT, or credit terms.

4. We Prepare

Your order is freshly processed, vacuum-sealed, and packaged with care.

5. Delivery

We deliver to your location or you collect from our Bloemfontein farm gate.

6. Enjoy!

Cook and enjoy fresh, quality poultry. Reorder anytime for ongoing supply.

Recipe Ideas

Get inspired with these delicious ways to prepare our products.

South African Braai Chicken

Marinate whole chicken with lemon, garlic, and your favourite spices. Grill over medium coals for 45-60 minutes, basting frequently.

Best with: Whole Chicken
Spicy Chicken Curry

Use chicken thighs for a rich, flavourful curry. Slow cook with onions, tomatoes, curry powder, and potatoes for a hearty family meal.

Best with: Thighs or Drumsticks
Grilled Chicken Wings

Toss wings in your favourite sauce and grill until crispy. Perfect for game days, parties, or a quick family dinner.

Best with: Chicken Wings
Chicken Breast Salad

Grill or pan-fry breast fillets and serve over fresh greens with avocado, cherry tomatoes, and a lemon dressing.

Best with: Breast Fillets
Breakfast Sausages

Pan-fry chicken sausages until golden. Serve with eggs, toast, and grilled tomatoes for a hearty breakfast.

Product FAQ

Store fresh chicken in the refrigerator at 0-4°C. Keep it in its original packaging or a sealed container on the bottom shelf to prevent cross-contamination. Use within 3-7 days of purchase. For longer storage, freeze at -18°C for up to 3 months.

Our chickens are raised locally in the Free State with no antibiotics or hormones. They are processed fresh daily (not frozen and thawed), and delivered directly from farm to you. You're getting farm-fresh quality at competitive prices.
