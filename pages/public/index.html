Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know about ordering from the farm.

Yes, all our poultry products are fresh and processed daily. Our slaughtering and processing facility follows strict quality controls, giving you complete peace of mind.

We deliver across Bloemfontein and the broader Free State. Farm gate pickup is free at our Bloemfontein location. Local delivery is R50, and regional delivery is quoted based on distance and quantity.

Minimum orders vary by customer type: Consumers can order from 1kg per product. Restaurants need 5kg minimum, retailers 10kg, and distributors 50kg per product.

Absolutely! Orders over 100kg get 5% off, 500kg+ gets 10% off, and 1000kg+ gets 15% off. These discounts apply on top of our already competitive wholesale pricing.

Our products are processed fresh daily. Whole chickens have a shelf life of 3-7 days, and value-added products last 4-7 days when stored at 0-4°C. We recommend consuming within 3 days for optimal freshness.

We accept cash on delivery (local orders), bank transfer/EFT with 50% deposit, and credit terms for established customers (30 days). Contact us to set up a payment arrangement.
