About Bohloko Family Farm

A community-based agricultural enterprise dedicated to producing affordable, high-quality poultry products for the Free State and beyond.

Our Mission

To strengthen food security, create local employment, and stimulate the township economy through sustainable poultry production. We aim to provide affordable access to quality protein while building a profitable, scalable agricultural enterprise.

Our Vision

To become the Free State's leading poultry producer, creating sustainable employment and contributing to local economic development. We envision a future where every community has access to fresh, affordable, locally-produced poultry products.

Our Story

Bohloko Family Farm - Poultry Department is a community-based agricultural enterprise located in the Free State, South Africa. We focus on the production, slaughtering, processing, and distribution of high-quality poultry products for Bloemfontein, surrounding townships, and the broader Free State market.

The project aims to strengthen food security, create local employment, and stimulate the township economy through sustainable poultry production.

The Challenge

Bloemfontein and surrounding townships experience high unemployment, rising food prices, and limited participation in agricultural value chains. Most poultry consumed in the area is transported from other provinces, leading to high costs, unstable supply, and limited local economic benefit.

There is a critical need for a locally rooted poultry production and processing enterprise that ensures affordable access to quality protein.

Our Solution

Bohloko Family Farm addresses these challenges by establishing a certified slaughtering and processing facility that produces affordable, high-quality poultry products.

  • Affordable, quality poultry for all
  • Local job creation in townships
  • Strengthened food security
  • Sustainable farming practices
  • Youth and skills development
